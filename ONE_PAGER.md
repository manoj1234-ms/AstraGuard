# ASTRAGUARD — One Page Overview

Problem:
Emergency evacuations are planned under uncertainty and time pressure.

Solution:
ASTRAGUARD is an intelligent decision-support system that:
• predicts evacuation time
• enforces safety constraints
• simulates worst-case scenarios
• requires human approval for risky decisions

Technology:
• ML + rule-based safety
• scenario simulation
• explainability
• offline & degraded operation

Who It’s For:
• District & city disaster authorities
• Emergency command centers

Status:
• Working MVP
• Demo-ready
• Deployment-aware

Vision:
To make emergency decisions safer, clearer, and more reliable.
